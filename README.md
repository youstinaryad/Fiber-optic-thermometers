Fiber Optic Thermometers & Temperature Measurement Systems
Technical study and performance analysis of fiber optic-based temperature sensors, examining optical pyrometry, fluorescence decay, and distributed temperature sensing (DTS) principles.
Key Objectives
Sensor Principles: Analyze how optical fiber characteristics (such as Bragg gratings or Raman scattering) change in response to temperature variations.
Measurement & Calibration: Evaluate response time, measurement accuracy, and calibration curves of fiber optic thermal sensors.
Industrial Applications: Investigate the advantages of optical thermometry in harsh environments, high-voltage equipment, and electromagnetic interference (EMI) fields.
Tools & Contents
Tools: Optical Spectrum Analyzers (OSA), Fiber Bragg Grating (FBG) sensors, Data Acquisition Systems.
Files Included: Full technical report, calibration curves, and analytical calculations (PDF).
